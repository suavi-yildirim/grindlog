Grindlog

A job application tracker that makes applying feel like progress. You earn XP for the work you put in, not for the answers you get back.

Open the tracker Free, no account, runs in your browser.

How it stays kind

Levels rise every 100 XP. The weekly goal fills one segment per effort step, counted from Monday.

There are no streaks, so a missed day takes nothing away. Quiet weeks are fine. The goal starts over each week, and the log keeps every step you took.

Private by default

The tracker runs in your browser and keeps your data there. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed. Export a JSON backup whenever you like.
