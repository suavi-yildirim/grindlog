    How XP works

    • Applied10
    • Tailored5
    • Followed up5
    • Interview25
    • Offer100
    • Rejected0

    Rejections cost nothing. Levels rise every 100 XP. There are no streaks, so a missed day takes nothing away. The goal starts over each week, and the log keeps every step you took.

    Private by default

    Your data stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to the public site and no account is needed. When you run the local server, saves also go to your private data repo. Export a JSON backup whenever you like, and keep exports out of any public repo.
